Goblet squat: 3 × 6–10
Romanian deadlift: 2 × 8–10
Split squat: 2 × 8 per leg
Calf raises: 3 × 12–15

Use manageable loads, stop with 2–3 reps in reserve, and avoid max-effort lifting. Thursday leg training should be moderate so Saturday jogging remains comfortable.
